Your own Warcraft Logs client

Every lookup that is not already cached spends Warcraft Logs points. On this instance you share a small hourly budget with the other members; with your own client — free, two minutes to create — you get Warcraft Logs' full budget for yourself and never wait for the hour to reset.

100 pts / hshared budget, per member · about 10 uncached lookups
3 600 pts / hyour own client · about 360 lookups, nobody else on it
On warcraftlogs.com
  1. Sign in at warcraftlogs.com — any free account works.
  2. Open warcraftlogs.com/api/clients (avatar → Clients) and click Create Client.
  3. Name it anything (say bmpl), set Redirect URLs to http://localhost, leave Public Client? unchecked, submit.
  4. Copy the Client ID and the Client Secret — the secret is shown once.
In bmpl
  1. Open your menu → Settings → Warcraft Logs client, paste both, Save and verify.
  2. Done: the quota line in your menu now shows your own counter, and your lookups no longer touch the shared budget.

The secret is stored encrypted and only ever sent to Warcraft Logs. If it leaks, delete the client on the same page and create a new one.
