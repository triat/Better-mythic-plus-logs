What bmpl looks at
  • Warcraft Logs rankings — the character's best run per dungeon this season, with parse %, key level and date. Fetched at every lookup; a lookup is kept 6 hours in your history.
  • Each run's log — deaths, damage taken, avoidable damage, interrupts, dispels, consumables, for the player and the four others in the group. Fetched once per run (~10 pts) and cached forever.
  • Raider.IO — item level, current and previous season score, last 10 runs. Free, no key.
All of it is public data: anyone can open the same logs on warcraftlogs.com and count the same deaths.
The verdict
The global score is the weighted mean of the axes that could be scored, with the weights of the player's role, rounded to a whole number. INVITE from 70, MAYBE from 45, PASS below. With fewer than 3 enriched runs the verdict is INSUFFICIENT DATA whatever the score. Confidence is high from 6 runs, medium from 3, low below.

FAQ
Why is Utility n/a for this rogue?
Rogues, warriors and death knights have no dispel; when a spec has neither a kick nor a dispel the axis has nothing to score. Healers are always scored.
Why does the parse say "unranked"?
A 0% parse means Warcraft Logs has not ranked that log yet; it is excluded from every parse-based signal instead of counting as a bad parse.
The score differs from the Raider.IO score — which is right?
They measure different things: Raider.IO scores how high the keys were, bmpl scores how the player played in them. A high RIO with a low bmpl score is a player carried through keys.
Why does a depleted key not count against them?
A depleted key is usually the group's fault. It is shown in the run list as context, never scored.
