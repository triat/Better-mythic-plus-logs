Key-level scaling
A death at +25 is not a death at +10. For the death-based sub-signals each run's count is multiplied by the factor below, read at that run's own key level (not the target), before the curve. Between two points the factor is interpolated; outside them it is clamped.
key level → factor
+8→ ×1.6
+12→ ×1.3
+16→ ×1.0
+20→ ×0.8
+25→ ×0.65
